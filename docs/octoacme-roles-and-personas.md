# OctoAcme Personas

This `octoacme-roles-and-personas.md` document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- Partner with Product Managers to clarify scope, acceptance criteria, and trade-offs
- Work with Project Managers on estimates, dependencies, delivery risks, and status
- Coordinate with QA/Testing Leads and Technical Leads on quality, design, and release readiness

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- Collaborate with Developers to refine scope, clarify requirements, and validate proposed solutions
- Work with Project Managers on priorities, milestones, risks, and stakeholder alignment
- Partner with Business Analysts and QA/Testing Leads to ensure requirements and acceptance criteria are testable

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- Coordinate with Developers on delivery plans, blockers, and technical risks
- Align with Product Managers on scope, timeline trade-offs, and stakeholder messaging
- Escalate decisions and risks to Sponsors or Executive Stakeholders when needed

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors or Executive Stakeholders own business outcomes, approve major investments, and provide escalation authority for high-impact project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Allocate budget and approve major project investments
- Make go/no-go decisions at key milestones
- Ensure project goals stay aligned with strategic business priorities
- Resolve escalations related to scope, resourcing, or business risk

### Goals
- Achieve measurable business outcomes from the project
- Maintain strategic alignment across teams and leadership
- Enable timely decisions when delivery risks or trade-offs arise

### Typical Communication
- Executive status reviews and milestone check-ins
- Risk escalation discussions with Project Managers
- Resource and priority decisions with Product Managers and delivery leads

### Key Interactions
- Receive project status, risks, and escalation requests from Project Managers
- Align with Product Managers on business value, priority shifts, and success measures
- Support Developers and Technical Leads indirectly by removing business or funding blockers

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA or Testing Leads define the quality strategy, coordinate validation activities, and provide confidence that deliverables meet acceptance expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define the QA approach, test coverage expectations, and validation checkpoints
- Create or coordinate test plans for functional, integration, and release testing
- Validate that acceptance criteria are complete, testable, and met
- Provide quality sign-off before release when required

### Goals
- Reduce defects that reach production
- Ensure acceptance criteria are validated consistently
- Create clear quality visibility throughout delivery

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions with Developers and Product Managers
- Defect triage, test result summaries, and sign-off updates
- Release-readiness coordination before deployment

### Key Interactions
- Work with Developers to coordinate test environments, defect fixes, and verification
- Partner with Product Managers and Business Analysts to confirm acceptance criteria are complete and testable
- Keep Project Managers informed about quality risks, test status, and release readiness

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical strategy, review designs, and make system-level decisions that balance speed, quality, and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architectural guardrails
- Review solution designs and implementation approaches
- Make or facilitate architectural decisions across teams
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and long-term maintainability concerns

### Goals
- Deliver scalable and supportable technical solutions
- Reduce technical risk through early design review and guidance
- Help teams make informed trade-offs between speed and complexity

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Implementation guidance during planning and execution
- Trade-off discussions with Product Managers and Project Managers

### Key Interactions
- Guide Developers on implementation patterns, design choices, and technical risk mitigation
- Partner with Product Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and product trade-offs
- Support Project Managers with technical risk assessment, dependency planning, and escalation input

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, manage release readiness, and ensure production changes are executed with clear communication and rollback planning.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release timelines, deployment windows, and readiness checkpoints
- Orchestrate deployment activities across staging and production environments
- Maintain rollback procedures and release checklists
- Communicate release scope, status, and outcomes to stakeholders

### Goals
- Deliver releases predictably with minimal risk
- Improve visibility and coordination during deployment windows
- Ensure teams are ready to respond quickly if issues occur after release

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and deployment schedules
- Readiness checklists, release notes, and rollback plans
- Post-deployment verification updates and stakeholder announcements

### Key Interactions
- Coordinate with Developers on deployment readiness, fixes, and post-release verification
- Align with Product Managers on release scope, timing, and customer-impacting changes
- Work with Project Managers on cross-team scheduling, dependencies, and release communications

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather, document, and trace requirements so teams can translate stakeholder needs into clear, actionable delivery plans.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit requirements from stakeholders and delivery teams
- Document user needs, workflows, and acceptance criteria
- Maintain traceability between business requirements and delivered work
- Validate that documented needs remain accurate as scope evolves

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and handoffs
- Improve traceability from stakeholder need to delivered outcome
- Support faster decisions by keeping documentation current and clear

### Typical Communication
- Discovery sessions, requirement reviews, and clarification follow-ups
- Documentation updates for workflows, stories, and acceptance criteria
- Stakeholder validation discussions before delivery commitments are finalized

### Key Interactions
- Partner with Product Managers to organize and prioritize documented requirements
- Support Developers by clarifying workflows, business rules, and edge cases
- Help Project Managers maintain scope clarity, decision records, and stakeholder alignment

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Agile Coaches support delivery teams by facilitating ceremonies, removing blockers, and improving the health and effectiveness of team processes.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help remove blockers that slow delivery
- Coach teams on process improvements and healthy collaboration habits
- Surface capacity concerns, workflow inefficiencies, and team health signals

### Goals
- Improve delivery flow and team predictability
- Strengthen continuous improvement and healthy team habits
- Create an environment where blockers are identified and resolved quickly

### Typical Communication
- Team ceremonies, facilitation notes, and follow-up actions
- Blocker tracking and process improvement discussions
- Capacity and workflow conversations with delivery leads

### Key Interactions
- Support Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers through facilitation and process coaching
- Partner with Project Managers on capacity planning, dependency visibility, and delivery flow
- Reinforce retrospective actions and continuous improvement with the full cross-functional team
